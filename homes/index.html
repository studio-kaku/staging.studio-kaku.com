In conversation

Three artists,
in their own words

Painting · Tokyo

Her paintings begin with water - vats of vivid colour poured over canvas laid flat on the floor. The pools dry, are sanded back, and new colour is added. Some layers disappear entirely. Others surface as thin traces, like light through amber. She works slowly. Each piece takes months.

Calligraphy · Okinawa

He grew up in Okinawa with calligraphy as a given. What changed was finding, much later, that the mark of a brush held something beyond language. His recent work uses light - projections, reflections, the quality of a surface - to extend the practice into something spatial. He describes it as writing without the need to be read.

Moss · Kamakura

Norihiko Kamei works with moss, dried flowers, and organic material in ways that blur the line between arrangement and installation. His studio in Kamakura is a world entirely its own, textured, living, quietly extraordinary.

How it works

Simple from your end.
A lot behind the scenes.

Every project starts with a conversation. From there, we handle the sourcing, the framing, and the installation. Here is how a typical project unfolds.

01

We talk

A short call to understand your home, your taste, and what you are hoping for. No commitment — we just want to know if it is a good fit.

02

We visit

We come to the space. We look at the light, the scale, the things you already have. This visit is what makes the curation personal rather than generic.

03

We propose

A curated selection of art, objects, and furniture — presented privately. You tell us what feels right. We refine until it does.

04

We install

We handle everything from framing to final placement. You come home to a space that feels like it was always meant to be that way.
