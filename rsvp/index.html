Onsen & Baths

Sawada Park Open-Air Onsen ↗

Open-air baths right above the sea, out in Nishiizu and worth the drive.

A rustic riverside spot in the forest, reached over a little wooden bridge.

Lie out on heated pebbles in a robe, a good one to slow right down in.

Kannon Onsen 観音温泉

Hirizo Beach ↗ ヒリゾ浜

Booking alone, or with fewer people than a room sleeps

This is the hotel's own pricing policy, not an extra charge from us; the per-person rate simply goes up to cover the cost of the room:

  • — Solo in a 2-person room: the hotel charges double the per-person rate (the same total they'd get from two people). This is set by the hotel, not by us. E.g. a Friday night ocean view twin room solo is ¥28,200 total, all in.
  • — 2 people in a 4-person room (e.g. two friends and a baby in the Quadruple room): each of the 2 paying adults pays double the per-person rate. If 3 people share it, each pays 1.3× instead.
  • — Infants & young children: an extra ¥5,000 per child, per night, on top of the room rate.
