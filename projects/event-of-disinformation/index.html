Talk on Disinformation

Maya Sobchuk at UltraSuperNew KURA

February 2026

Speaker

Maya Sobchuk

Venue

UltraSuperNew KURA

Curation

Studio Kaku

A talk by writer and researcher Maya Sobchuk at UltraSuperNew KURA, exploring how the information consumed by the public has been quietly and deliberately shaped by unseen forces. Spanning multiple mediums, technologies and historical perspectives, Sobchuk examined the disconnect between creative and intellectual communities — and why artists are essential to addressing the problems of the modern internet.
