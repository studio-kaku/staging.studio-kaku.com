The spaces

What the team delivers
across the resort

Three spaces with different briefs and different audiences. The team structure means all of them feel like the same resort.

01

The villas

Available to any owner who wants it. Art, furniture, and local goods chosen for the space, the budget, and the owner's taste - rental-appropriate where needed. Studio Kaku manages every owner conversation.

02

The gallery

A gallery space built around the family's story, with a rotating programme of contemporary Japanese art. Studio Kaku runs the programme and keeps it current each season.

03

The members lounge

A space for villa owners to actually use - not a hotel lobby. Art, furniture, and craft objects chosen so people want to spend time in this unique environment. This is where the resort community forms.

The members lounge

What your members
lounge could look like

A space that feels like a home, not a lobby. Owners can actually use it. Every object is there for a reason.

The feeling

Somewhere between a collector's home and a private members club. Warm and specific. Materials that reference Hokkaido without being touristy about it.

The art

A small permanent collection anchors the space - two or three pieces the owners get to know over time. A rotating element keeps it fresh each season.

The objects

Local craft alongside contemporary design. Nothing is there to fill a gap - every piece is chosen because it earns attention or starts a conversation.

The gallery

What your gallery
could look like

A gallery rooted in the family's story, open to the broader Japanese art world. Something that gives owners a concrete reason to come back each season.

The programme

Two or three exhibitions per year, each running across a season. One anchored in the family's heritage, one open to emerging Japanese artists, one tied to Hokkaido specifically.

The collection

A permanent collection builds over time - works acquired through the programme that belong to the resort. This is what gives the gallery a real point of view.

For owners

Villa owners get early access to editions, invitations to openings, and the option to acquire works for their own spaces. The gallery becomes a reason to be part of this resort community.

The villas

What your villa
could look like

Each villa is different. We work within the space, the budget, and the owner's taste, choosing art, furniture, and objects that feel like they belong there.

The feel

Japanese-influenced and locally grounded. Warm without being rustic. Each villa gets a distinct point of view, not a template.

The art

Chosen for the scale and light of the specific space. Original works, limited editions, and framed pieces, never generic hotel prints.

For rental use

Where owners use the villa for rental income, we select pieces that work for both personal use and guests, durable, beautiful, and replaceable where needed.

How it works for owners

The catalogue

We assemble a catalogue of 200 pieces - interior design objects and works of art, each chosen to work in these villas. Depending on which package an owner chooses, they get a browsable edit of 60 pieces, a personal shortlist we build around their space, or full catalogue access with sourcing beyond it.

200

pieces in the catalogue

Interior objects, art prints, original works, and craft pieces - built up over years of relationships, chosen with these villas in mind.

Curated
Plan

A personal edit, built around your space

We talk first - your space, how you use it, what you like. Then we go through the full catalogue and put together a shortlist of 15-25 pieces for your villa. You get a filtered edit, not 200 options to sort through yourself.

  • - Consultation with Studio Kaku
  • - Access to the full 200-piece catalogue
  • - Personal edit of 15–25 curated pieces
  • - Selection rationale for each piece
  • - Installation guidance included

Commissioned
Plan

The catalogue is a starting point

For owners who want something genuinely unique. We go beyond the catalogue - reaching out to makers, commissioning pieces for the specific space, sourcing work that isn't widely available. Everything chosen for your villa.
