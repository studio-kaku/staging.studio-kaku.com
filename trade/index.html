In conversation

Three artists,
in their own words

Painting · Tokyo

Her paintings begin with water - vats of vivid colour poured over canvas laid flat on the floor. The pools dry, are sanded back, and new colour is added. Some layers disappear entirely. Others surface as thin traces, like light through amber. She works slowly. Each piece takes months.

Calligraphy · Okinawa

He grew up in Okinawa with calligraphy as a given. What changed was finding, much later, that the mark of a brush held something beyond language. His recent work uses light - projections, reflections, the quality of a surface - to extend the practice into something spatial. He describes it as writing without the need to be read.

Ceramics

She fires clay until it cracks, then mends it. The cracks are filled with glaze and fired again - each repair becoming part of the surface, not hidden from it. The pieces carry a record of what happened to them. She is interested in the idea that something broken and restored is not diminished - it is more itself.
