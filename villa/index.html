Every object we curate has a story and an artist behind it. These are some of the people Studio Kaku works with - their knowledge of Hokkaido's materials and traditions is built into everything they produce.

Three generations · Hokkaido

Taisetsugama

Where the landscape enters the glaze

A ceramics studio rooted entirely in its surroundings. Taisetsugama works across three series: hand-painted pieces depicting the hills and skies of Biei; a crystalline glaze technique passed down across three generations, where snow and ice formations emerge in the firing - never guaranteed, always unique; and a semi-porcelain line in warm ivory with vivid colour glazes that evoke freshly fallen snow in sunlight. Each piece is designed to feel as good in the hand as it looks on a shelf.

Painted ceramics · Crystalline glaze · Semi-porcelain · Bespoke vessels

Third generation · Est. 1906

Asahikawa & Sapporo

A century of Hokkaido craft

Founded in 1906 as a furniture shop in Asahikawa, this family-run gallery has been shaped by the Mingei folk craft movement for over a century. Now in its third generation, it has grown into a multi-location operation - Asahikawa, Sapporo, and the Shin-Furano Prince Hotel.

Tableware · Everyday objects · Clothing · Paintings · Prints · Sculpture

Higashikawa, Hokkaido

From log to finished piece, in-house

A Japanese furniture and interiors brand with a factory in Higashikawa - a town where roughly 40% of residents are involved in woodworking. Around 30 craftsmen hand-finish each piece using locally sourced timber, with machinery kept to a minimum.
