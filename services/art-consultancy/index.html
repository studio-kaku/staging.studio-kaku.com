Art consultancy

Art consultancy in Tokyo

We help people and organisations find art that belongs in their space, then take care of everything that follows. Studio Kaku works with first-time buyers, collectors, offices and developers, drawing on a network of Japanese and international artists. Clients include Mitsui Fudosan, Soho House and Tokyo Creative Salon at Haneda Airport.

What we do

Curation

A curatorial direction for each space: what the art should do there, and why. Not a mood board, but a point of view.

Sourcing & commissions

Existing works, or new pieces commissioned for your space. We handle negotiation, contracts and timelines.

Acquisition advice

Honest guidance on what you are buying, whether it is your first piece or a growing collection.

Framing, installation & handover

Museum-standard framing and installation, with full documentation of artists, provenance and care.
