Hotels & hospitality

Art curation for hotels

Art shapes how guests remember a stay. We curate, source, frame and install art for hotels, ryokan and hospitality spaces, from a single lobby or restaurant to every room in the property, and manage it all as your one point of contact. Past work includes the art curation for Mitsui Fudosan's Sequence hotels in Kyoto and Tokyo, and projects with Soho House.
