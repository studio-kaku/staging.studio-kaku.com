Framing

Custom art framing in Tokyo

Every work is framed to museum standard, using archival materials chosen for that piece. We frame for private collectors, artists, galleries and companies across Tokyo, from a single print or a child's drawing to a full exhibition. We work in collaboration with Juka Framing, the workshop founded by our co-founder Yuki Kakuno. Past framing includes work for Ceysson & Bénétière's Ginza gallery, the Tokyo American Club, Toranomon Hills and GINZA SIX.

What we frame

Classic framing with mats

Window mats cut to suit the work, with archival boards that protect it over time.

Floating frames

The work is raised inside the frame so its edges stay visible. Ideal for deckle-edged paper.

Acrylic boxes

Clear boxes for three-dimensional pieces and objects.

Canvas frames & stretching

L-shaped frames for canvases, and stretching for paintings that arrive rolled.

Glass & acrylic glazing

Standard glass, non-reflective glass or acrylic, chosen for the work and where it will hang.

Questions

Do you frame for private individuals, or only galleries?

+

Both. We frame for private collectors, artists, galleries and companies, from a single print or photograph to a whole exhibition.

What do you need from me for a quote?

+

The size of the work and a photo. Send both through our enquiry form and we will reply with frame, mat and glazing options and a quote.

Do you use archival, museum-standard materials?

+

Yes. Every work is framed with archival materials chosen for that piece, with standard glass, non-reflective glass or acrylic depending on the work and where it will hang.

Can you deliver and hang the framed work?

+

Yes. We can deliver the finished piece and install it for you.

Can we talk in English?

+

Yes. We work in both English and Japanese.
